The problem

Screens blind.
Then somebody gets called out.

Clogging

Wedge wire and mesh blind on fats and fibres. Capacity drops before anyone notices.

Backwash

Spray bars need clean water, pressure and nozzles that block. Water you treat twice.

Three machines

Screening, thickening and dewatering usually means three units, three drives, three spares lists.

The solution

One machine,
three duties.

Thickener

Cuts sludge volume ahead of storage, digestion or a dewatering step.

Dewatering

Takes the sludge through to a transportable cake.

Screen

Primary screening of raw wastewater, up to 167 m³/h per unit.

Special oval discs sit on parallel shafts and rotate in continuous synchronised motion. Solids ride across the deck; liquid passes through the slits between the discs.

Because neighbouring discs are always moving relative to each other, the slit is wiped on every turn. The structure is self-cleaning: no spray bar, no backwash line, no filter media to replace. Low rotation speed means no vibration and no noise.

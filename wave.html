The solution

One machine,
three duties.

Thickener

Cuts sludge volume ahead of storage, digestion or a dewatering step.

Dewatering

Takes the sludge through to a transportable cake.

Screen

Primary screening of raw wastewater, up to 167 m³/h per unit.

Special oval discs sit on parallel shafts and rotate in continuous synchronised motion. Solids ride across the deck; liquid passes through the slits between the discs.

Because neighbouring discs are always moving relative to each other, the slit is wiped on every turn. The structure is self-cleaning: no spray bar, no backwash line, no filter media to replace. Low rotation speed means no vibration and no noise.

Why Wave Separator

Six reasons
it earns its footprint.

  • 01MultifunctionalOne feed box, three duties: screen raw wastewater, thicken sludge or dewater it to cake. The same platform, set up for the job.
  • 02No cloggingOval discs turn in step between the slits, so every turn wipes them. Fat, hair and fibre ride over the deck instead of blinding it.
  • 03Large transport loadThe discs carry the solids along the deck to the chute. In dewatering duty a pressure plate holds them back until the cake leaves at 25% solids or better.
  • 04No backwashLiquid falls through the 1 mm slits into the trough and leaves by the flanges. No spray bar, no clean-water line, no nozzles.
  • 05Energy saving0.1 to 2.25 kW across the range. Slow, synchronised rotation: no vibration, no noise, no dynamic foundation.
  • 06Stable, easy to runThroughput holds because there is no clean cycle to wait for. Few moving parts and an open deck keep maintenance routine.

Slit and duty

The gap is
the setting.

01

1 mm as standard

The slit between the discs ships at 1 mm and is set to the job. Screening, thickening and dewatering each want a different gap.

02

0.5% to 10% as a thickener

On thickening duty it takes feed from 0.5% to 10% solids and hands a smaller, richer flow to whatever dewaters downstream.

03

25% cake on dewatering duty

With the pressure plate fitted, the discharged cake leaves at 25% dry solids minimum.

Custom slit widths are available. Send the application, not the model number.

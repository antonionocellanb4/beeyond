The solution

Progressive dewatering
inside one drum.

The dewatering drum is built from interlaminated fixed rings and free rings, held by rods with spacers in between. A screw turns slowly through the stack.

Sludge is thickened and then dewatered progressively as it travels along the drum. Filtrate escapes through the gaps between the rings. The screw pitch and the space between the rings get smaller toward the end plate, so pressure builds where the sludge is already thick — and the cake is discharged at the end plate.

  • AFixed ringsForm the filtration slot and carry the load.
  • BFree ringsRide with the screw and keep the slot open — the self-cleaning effect.
  • CScrewLow speed, narrowing pitch. Transport plus compression.
  • DEnd plateBack pressure point. Sets cake dryness.
Why MDS®

Six reasons
it stays running.

  • 01Wide concentration range2 000 to 50 000 mg/L through the same inlet, on the same machine.
  • 02Clog-free, no thickenerMoving rings wipe the gaps between the fixed rings on every turn, so nothing blinds. Thickening happens in the drum: no thickener upstream.
  • 03Low speed, low powerScrew pitch and ring gap narrow toward the outlet, so pressure builds slowly. 0.2 to 8.2 kW, no vibration, no foundation.
  • 04Self-cleaningFiltrate leaves through the ring gaps while they clean themselves. Rinsing water in litres per hour: 24 L/h on an MDS101.
  • 05Replaces existing kitCake at 20% dry solids or better. Drops in for a belt press, decanter centrifuge or filter press.
  • 06Fully automatic controlPressure builds against the end plate and the cake leaves on its own. Unattended start, run and washdown.

Built to be serviced

Wear you can
see coming.

01

Tungsten-carbide screw

The screw flight is carbide-coated, and the rings ride on its crest instead of scraping it. No metal-on-metal wear to replace.

02

One-button opening

The cylinder opens on a single lever. You can watch the thickening and dewatering zones while the machine runs, and adjust from what you see.

03

Reference ring

A marked ring sits in each zone. Compare it against the rest to schedule an overhaul, instead of waiting for the slot to drift out of tolerance.

04

Pre-thickening module

An optional thickening stage inside the flocculation tank. On sludge below 1% dry solids it lifts throughput by more than half.

Optional module. Say so at sizing — it changes the model you need.

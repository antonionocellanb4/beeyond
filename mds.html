SCREW PRESS

Advanced sludge dewatering. One low-speed screw, a stack of fixed and free rings, and a cake you can shovel.

The problem

Sludge is 97% water
and you pay to move all of it.

Volume

Liquid sludge fills tankers fast. Every m³ hauled is water you already paid to treat.

Handling

Belt presses need wash water and an operator. Centrifuges need power and a service contract.

Cost

Disposal is billed by weight. Polymer, energy and downtime sit on top.

The solution

Progressive dewatering
inside one drum.

The dewatering drum is built from interlaminated fixed rings and free rings, held by rods with spacers in between. A screw turns slowly through the stack.

Sludge is thickened and then dewatered progressively as it travels along the drum. Filtrate escapes through the gaps between the rings. The screw pitch and the space between the rings get smaller toward the end plate, so pressure builds where the sludge is already thick — and the cake is discharged at the end plate.

  • AFixed ringsForm the filtration slot and carry the load.
  • BFree ringsRide with the screw and keep the slot open — the self-cleaning effect.
  • CScrewLow speed, narrowing pitch. Transport plus compression.
  • DEnd plateBack pressure point. Sets cake dryness.

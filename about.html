BEEYONDWater technology

Getting the water
out of the solids.

Beeyond supplies two machines for wastewater and sludge: a multi-disk screw press and a rotating oval-disc separator. We size them on your dry-solids load, install them, start them, and hold the wear parts.

Two products instead of forty is a deliberate limit. It is what lets us answer a sizing question in a phone call, and say plainly when neither machine is the right answer.

What you are buying
01

Sizing on data

Sludge type, concentration, kilos of dry solids an hour, hours a day. The answer is one model number and the reasoning behind it — not a range that covers everything and commits to nothing.

02

Machines that run alone

No filter medium to blind, no backwash line, no operator standing by with a hose. Both machines start on a timer, run through the night and get checked on your round rather than on a callout.

03

Parts and people after

Rings, seals and scrapers in stock. The engineer who sized the unit is the one who commissions it, trains your operator and picks up the phone two years later.

Our values
A

Honest sizing

If neither machine suits your sludge we say so, and you keep your money. A wrong machine costs us more in service than the order was worth.

B

Fewer moving parts

Every component we can leave out is one that cannot fail at three in the morning. Low speed, no spray bars, no filter media in the spares list.

C

Present after the sale

Commissioning, operator training and spares are part of the deal, not a line item added once the machine is on the floor.

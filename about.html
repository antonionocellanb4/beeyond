Why Beeyond

Beeyond was born
from experience.

For nearly forty years, Ecoimpianti Sud has been designing, building and operating municipal and industrial wastewater treatment plants. Working every day on real plants, under real operating conditions, has built extensive know-how across the entire water cycle – from process engineering to operation, maintenance and troubleshooting.

Over the years, one challenge became increasingly clear: sludge management is one of the key factors determining the efficiency and operating cost of a wastewater treatment plant. This experience led us to look beyond conventional solutions.

We tested and operated several sludge dewatering technologies, comparing their performance directly in the field and identifying the solutions capable of delivering the best combination of efficiency, reliability, simplicity and operating cost.

This is where Beeyond comes from: a dedicated brand focused on advanced technologies for sludge and digestate treatment, combining field experience with carefully selected and optimized solutions.
